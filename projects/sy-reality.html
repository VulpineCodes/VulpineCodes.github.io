Sy-Reality!

VR karaoke for Quest 3 — a stage, a spotlight, and lyrics that keep time with you.

Sy-Reality! is a VR karaoke experience built in Unity for Meta Quest 3: step into an immersive performance environment, pick a song, and perform with a lyric-sync engine driving timed cues. Songs are data-driven definitions rather than hard-coded scenes, and the build is in active development as the immersive counterpart to my lab research.

Why it exists

At the Wallace Multisensory Lab I built VR paradigms for neuroscience — precise, controlled, and deliberately sterile. Sy-Reality! is the other direction: the same real-time craft pointed at joy. Karaoke is performance, nerves, and delight; VR can put a stage under anyone.

It also grows out of my SyBBURE fellowship (the name is a nod) and everything the lab taught me about how people perceive and move through virtual spaces.

My role: Solo developer — Unity/C#, interaction design, lyric-sync engine, environment art direction.

Architecture

Engineering decisions & trade-offs

Songs as data, not scenes

Each song is a definition — timing track, lyric cues, environment hints — loaded by the engine. Adding a song never means rebuilding a scene, and the timing system can be tested deterministically outside the headset.

A real state machine for performance

Start, pause, resume, restart, and completion are explicit states with guarded transitions — the same discipline as my backend work, applied to a play session. Interrupted playback is a handled case, not a surprise.

Only licensed test content

Development uses original and public-domain test material. No copyrighted recordings or lyrics ship with the project.

Testing & evidence

  • Deterministic logic — song definitions, timing, and state transitions — is structured for automated tests outside the headset.
  • In-headset verification on Quest 3 hardware is part of the development loop for interaction and comfort.

Results

  • Playable vertical slice in development: environment, song flow, and lyric synchronization.
  • Architecture and interaction decisions documented in the project repository as the build progresses.

Honest limitations

  • In active development — the current build is a vertical slice, not a finished release.
  • No public gameplay footage yet; nothing on this page is a screenshot of unreleased features.
  • Pitch detection and vocal scoring are being evaluated as optional extensions and are not claimed as working features.

Status: In active development. The repository’s STATUS file tracks what is verified versus pending, in the open.
