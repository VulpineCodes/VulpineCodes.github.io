Chapters of Change

Donating comics should be as easy as loving them.

Chapters of Change moves donated comics and manga from people's shelves to schools and libraries. Donors list what they have, organizations request what they need, and a strict approval workflow carries each donation from offer to delivered. Each donation is tracked through approval to delivery, and a retried submission creates one record, not two.

Full donor to recipient flow verified over real HTTP against the booted API.

AI Application Engineering

Myles the Travel Buddy

A travel planner that shows its work.

Myles the Travel Buddy builds travel itineraries from your budget and constraints, then proves every recommendation traces back to a labeled fixture record. A deterministic planner does the math, a validator enforces the rules, and an evaluation harness keeps score. The prices are sample data, and the page says so wherever they appear.

Both workflow implementations return the identical grounded itinerary from fixture data, verified down to record IDs and citations.

Data / Infrastructure

PharmaFlow

Serious data infrastructure for small pharmacies.

PharmaFlow is the operations platform small pharmacies rarely get: idempotent ingestion of inventory and sales, data-quality quarantine, analytics measured locally at under a second on synthetic data, streaming over a local event log that survives crash tests, and an API serving the local store. Sized for a family pharmacy, and built with the care that work deserves.

Ingestion near 7,000 rows per second at 50k rows, measured on a local single machine. Re-importing the same 50k rows takes 0.145 seconds, because the file hash says nothing changed.

Immersive · VR · In development

Sy-Reality!

A karaoke stage, inside a headset.

Sy-Reality! is a VR karaoke experience for Meta Quest 3. Pick a song, step onto a virtual stage, and perform while a lyric-sync engine drives every cue. Songs are data, not hard-coded scenes, so the timing core can be tested deterministically without putting on the headset.

Unity · C# · OpenXR · Meta Quest 3. The timing core is tested; the in-headset loop is in active development. Nothing here is staged footage.
