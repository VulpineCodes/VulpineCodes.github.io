Chapters of Change

A donation platform that moves comics and manga from donors to the kids who'll love them.

Chapters of Change connects people with comics and manga to spare with schools and libraries that want them. Donors list collections, organizations request them, and a structured approval workflow — with idempotent submissions and strict authorization — carries each donation from offer to delivered.

AI Application Engineering02

Myles

Travel intelligence that plans deterministically — and proves its answers are grounded.

Myles builds constraint-aware travel itineraries you can audit. A deterministic planner does the math, a validator enforces hard constraints, and a grounding layer checks that every claim in the output traces back to provider data. An evaluation harness scores the whole pipeline, so quality is measured rather than asserted.

  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • LangGraph

Data / Infrastructure03

PharmaFlow

Reliable data infrastructure for the small pharmacies that big software forgets.

PharmaFlow is a data platform for small pharmacies: idempotent ingestion of inventory and sales records, data-quality quarantine, analytics that run in under a second, event streaming that survives crashes without double-applying stock, and an API serving it all. Every claim in its documentation is backed by a test or a benchmark with its conditions recorded.

  • FastAPI
  • DuckDB

Immersive · VR · In development

Sy-Reality!

VR karaoke for Quest 3 — a stage, a spotlight, and lyrics that keep time with you.

Sy-Reality! is a VR karaoke experience built in Unity for Meta Quest 3: step into an immersive performance environment, pick a song, and perform with a lyric-sync engine driving timed cues. Songs are data-driven definitions rather than hard-coded scenes, and the build is in active development as the immersive counterpart to my lab research.

Step inside →
  • Lyric-sync engine — timed cues driven by data-driven song definitions, not hard-coded scenes.
  • Real state machine — start, pause, resume, restart, complete, with interrupted playback handled.
  • Lab-honed craft — built on the real-time discipline of neuroscience VR at the Wallace Lab.

Unity · C# · OpenXR · Meta Quest 3. A build in progress — no staged footage, no claimed features that aren’t in the build.
