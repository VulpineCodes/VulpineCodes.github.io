Lab / team work

The Cave — Wallace Multisensory Lab

Vanderbilt University · funded by Meta Reality Labs

The Cave is an immersive research facility combining 4K projection, motion capture, haptics, and spatial audio to study how the brain integrates the senses. As a software developer intern, I built VR experimental paradigms and reusable Unity task templates used across 10+ neuroscience experiments.

Lab work belongs to the lab: paradigms and data are the team's, and unpublished research material isn't shown here. What I can share is the craft — real-time systems that have to be precise, because an experiment is only as good as its timing.

  • —10+ neuroscience experiments supported
  • —4+ reusable Unity task templates
  • —4K projection · motion capture · haptics · spatial audio

Lab / team work

Privacy Policy Research — UVM SOCKS

University of Vermont · SOCKS research group

With the SOCKS group, I worked on the collection and analysis of privacy policies at scale — the unglamorous documents that decide what companies may do with your data. The analysis code is public in my privacypolicyanalysis repository. It's also where my instinct for reading systems critically — what does this actually do, versus what does it say — got sharper; that instinct shows up later in the privacy review work I did at Microsoft.

  • —Large-scale policy collection
  • —Computational analysis of policy text
  • —Public code: privacypolicyanalysis
