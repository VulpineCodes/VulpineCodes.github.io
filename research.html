Lab / team work

The Cave, Wallace Multisensory Lab

Vanderbilt University, funded by Meta Reality Labs

The Cave studies how the brain merges the senses: 4K projection, motion capture, haptics, spatial audio. I built the VR paradigms the experiments ran on. In a lab, a millisecond of timing error ruins a trial, so the code had to be exact.

Lab work belongs to the lab. The paradigms and the data are the team's, and unpublished material stays unpublished. What transfers is the craft: real-time systems built to be precise.

  • →10+ neuroscience experiments supported
  • →4+ reusable Unity task templates
  • →4K projection, motion capture, haptics, spatial audio

Fellowship

Vanderbilt

SyBBURE funds Vanderbilt undergrads to run independent immersive research projects, with mentoring and a research community around them. Mine centers on immersive technology. Sy-Reality! grew directly out of it.

Lab / team work

Privacy Policy Research, UVM SOCKS

University of Vermont, SOCKS research group

With the SOCKS group I worked on collecting and analyzing privacy policies at scale: the documents that decide what companies may do with your data. The code is public. It is also where I got sharper at reading systems critically, asking what something actually does versus what it says. That habit showed up later in the privacy review I did at Microsoft.

  • →Large-scale policy collection
  • →Computational analysis of policy text
  • →Public code: privacypolicyanalysis
